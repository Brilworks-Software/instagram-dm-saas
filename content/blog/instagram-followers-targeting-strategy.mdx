# Instagram Followers Targeting Strategy for Better Conversions

Targeting is more important than messaging.

If you target random users, conversion will be low.

## Target Competitor Followers

Find accounts in your niche and extract followers.

## Target Influencer Followers

Influencers attract people interested in specific topics.

## Filter Irrelevant Accounts

Remove:

- Empty profiles
- Fake accounts
- Inactive users

## Focus on Relevance

Relevance increases:

- Reply rate
- Trust
- Conversion

Quality beats quantity.
