# How to Connect Your Instagram Account to SocialOra (Step-by-Step Guide)

If you want to use Instagram automation for lead generation and outreach, the first step is connecting your Instagram account correctly.

Many users feel confused when they try to connect their account for the first time. They worry about security, account bans, or technical issues. This guide explains everything in simple language so you can connect your Instagram account to SocialOra safely and start using its features without problems.

By the end of this article, you will understand:

- How to install the SocialOra Chrome extension  
- How to connect your Instagram account  
- How to find leads using followers  
- How to create an outreach campaign  
- How to send messages from the inbox  
- How to use automation safely  

Let’s start from the beginning.

---

## Why SocialOra Uses a Chrome Extension

SocialOra connects to Instagram using a Chrome browser extension. This means:

- You log in directly on Instagram’s official website.
- SocialOra does not ask for your Instagram password inside the app.
- The connection works through your logged-in browser session.

This method is safer compared to tools that require you to enter your Instagram password directly into their platform.

If Instagram logs you out, you can simply reconnect again using the extension.

---

## Step 1: Install the SocialOra Chrome Extension

To connect your Instagram account, you first need to install the extension.

Follow these steps:

1. Log in to your SocialOra account.
2. Go to the **Instagram Accounts** tab.
3. Click on **Login with Instagram**.
4. If prompted, click **Go to Extension Page**.
5. Click **Add to Chrome**.
6. Confirm the installation.

After installation, you will see the SocialOra extension icon in your Chrome toolbar.

---

## Step 2: Connect Your Instagram Account

Once the extension is installed:

1. Go back to SocialOra.
2. Click **Connect with Extension**.
3. You will be redirected to the Instagram website.
4. Log in to your Instagram account if you are not already logged in.
5. Click the SocialOra extension icon in your browser.
6. Click **Grab Session**.

After a successful connection, you will automatically be redirected back to the SocialOra platform.

Your Instagram account is now connected.

---

## What Does "Grab Session" Mean?

When you click "Grab Session", the extension securely reads your logged-in Instagram browser session.

Important points:

- Your password is not stored inside SocialOra.
- You log in only on Instagram’s official website.
- The connection is based on your active session.

If Instagram logs you out, you just repeat this step.

---

## Step 3: Find Leads Using the Followers Tool

After connecting your account, you can start generating Instagram leads.

To find leads:

1. Go to the **Leads** tab.
2. Click on **Users Followers**.
3. Enter the username of the account you want to target.
4. Click **Lookup User**.

You will see the Instagram profile details.

To extract followers:

5. Click **Get Followers**.

This works only if the account is public. Private accounts cannot be accessed.

You can collect:

- Followers
- Following list

This helps you target users who are already interested in your niche or competitors.

---

## Step 4: Create an Instagram Outreach Campaign

Once you have collected leads, you can create a campaign.

Go to the **Campaigns** section and click **Create Campaign**.

### Campaign Step 1: Add Basic Details

- Enter a campaign name.
- Add a short description.

This helps you manage multiple campaigns easily.

---

### Campaign Step 2: Set Time Range

Choose when messages should be sent.

For example:

- 9 AM to 6 PM
- Only weekdays

This makes your automation look natural.

---

### Campaign Step 3: Set Daily Message Limit

Choose how many messages you want to send per day.

If your Instagram account is new, start with a low number.

Gradually increase the limit over time to reduce the risk of restrictions.

---

### Campaign Step 4: Select Target Leads

Choose the profiles you want to send messages to.

You can select:

- Individual users
- Multiple leads at once

---

### Campaign Step 5: Write and Personalize Your Message

Now write your message.

Keep it:

- Short
- Clear
- Personalized

Avoid spam words or aggressive sales messages.

You can personalize messages using usernames or custom variables. Personalized messages increase reply rates and reduce risk.

---

### Campaign Step 6: Review and Launch

Before launching, review:

- Message content
- Daily limits
- Time range
- Selected leads

If everything looks correct, launch your campaign.

Your Instagram outreach campaign is now active.

---

## Step 5: Send Messages from the Inbox

SocialOra also allows manual messaging.

To send a direct message:

1. Go to the **Inbox** screen.
2. Click **Start Conversation**.
3. Enter the username.
4. Type your message.
5. Click Send.

After the first message, you do not need to re-enter the username. You can continue chatting from the inbox directly.

This keeps all conversations organized in one place.

---

## Managing Your Connected Instagram Account

Inside the Leads section, you can see:

- Your connected Instagram username
- Campaign activity
- Lead status
- Message history

This helps you track your outreach performance.

---

## How to Stay Safe While Using Instagram Automation

Instagram automation should be used carefully.

Follow these best practices:

- Start with low daily message limits.
- Increase limits gradually.
- Personalize messages.
- Avoid sending identical messages to everyone.
- Send messages during natural hours.
- Reply to users who respond.

Automation should look like normal human behavior.

---

## Common Issues and Fixes

### Instagram Logs You Out

Solution:

- Log in again on Instagram.
- Click the extension.
- Click Grab Session again.

---

### Cannot Extract Followers

Possible reasons:

- The account is private.
- Temporary Instagram limitation.

Solution:

- Use public accounts.
- Try again later.

---

### Messages Not Sending

Possible reasons:

- Daily limit reached.
- Session expired.
- Instagram temporary block.

Solution:

- Reduce daily limits.
- Reconnect your account.
- Wait before retrying.
